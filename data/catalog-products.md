# Catalog products and Standard Price Book entries

Input file for Claude Code. Save it in the repo as `data/catalog-products.md`.
It defines every Product2 record and its Standard Price Book entry for the apple-gtm-cpq build.

## How to load it

- One row = one Product2 record + one PricebookEntry in the Standard Price Book (USD, active).
- `External ID` goes into both `ProductCode` and `AGTM_External_Id__c`. Upsert on `AGTM_External_Id__c` so reloads never duplicate.
- Device prices are the US **starting price** (base configuration). Variant prices (storage, size, chip, memory) live in `AGTM_Device_Price__c` and are applied by the lookup price rule (Phase 1 step 4), not here.
- Bundle parents are priced at $0. Their components carry the price.
- Blank subscription columns mean a one-time product (Subscription Pricing left empty).
- Recurring products need a Billing Type; CPQ rejects a Recurring product without one.
- Product Family values used here (iPhone, Apple Watch, Mac, Services, Accessories, Bundles) must exist on the `Product2.Family` picklist. Add any that are missing and deploy that first.

| Subscription Type    | `SBQQ__SubscriptionType__c`                      |
| Configuration Type   | `SBQQ__ConfigurationType__c`                     |
| Configuration Event  | `SBQQ__ConfigurationEvent__c`                    |
| List Price (USD)     | `PricebookEntry.UnitPrice` (Standard Price Book) |

## Notes

- **Not in this file:** the trade-in product (Phase 2 step 4, negative prices by model and condition), bundle features and options (Phase 1 step 5 and Phase 2 step 2), and the `AGTM_Device_Price__c` variant rows (Phase 1 step 4).
- **MacBook Pro is one product per size and chip**, the way Apple prices it: the chip sets the memory and storage ranges, GPU options and power adapter, so it is the base model, not an attribute. Remaining attributes for the pricing table: CPU/GPU variant within the chip, memory, storage, color and nano-texture display. Constraints such as 128GB requiring the 40-core M5 Max GPU are product rules.
- **MacBook Air configuration attributes** for the pricing table: GPU (8-core only on the 13-inch 16GB/512GB), memory, storage and color. Add Mac rows to `AGTM_Device_Price__c` the same way as iPhone and Watch.
- **Prices to re-check before a demo:** Apple raised Mac prices in March 2026 and again in June 2026. MacBook Pro rows use the post-June prices; MacBook Air rows still use March 2026 prices. Confirm current US starting prices on apple.com and update this table if they changed.
- **Build phase "Catalog only"** means the product is in the catalog for reps to quote, but no build step depends on it.
- **iPhone Duo** needs storage rows in `AGTM_Device_Price__c` (Phase 1 step 4): 256GB $1,999, 512GB $2,199, 1TB $2,599, 2TB $3,199. It is eSIM only, so it has no physical SIM option. AppleCare for Enterprise is one flat illustrative price for every device; a $1,999 foldable would normally cost more to cover.
- **Accessories** are the Apple-branded iPhone accessories on apple.com/shop/iphone/accessories (October 2026), one product each with color or length as an attribute. Left out: Beats and third-party products, AirPods and HomePod, and the Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter (no phone in this catalog has Lightning). The iPhone Air MagSafe Battery is included because Apple lists it there, but it fits no phone in this catalog.
- **Illustrative prices** (AppleCare for Enterprise, Device Management, Cellular Activation) are placeholders for the demo, not Apple's published enterprise pricing.
